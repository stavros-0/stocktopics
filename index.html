More LibreOffice spreadsheets have yet to be translated into the site.

The charts are for research and exploration, not investment advice.

Sources: Meta quarterly earnings releases for 2021–2024 revenue, net income, shares and EPS (per-quarter source links), and META.ods Financials and Cash sheets for the remaining data. R&D comes from the Financials sheet; Q4 2021–2024 amounts are full-year R&D less Q1–Q3. All cash-series values, including construction in progress and D&A, are copied from the Cash sheet. Cash, marketable securities and construction in progress are quarter-end balances. The quarterly change in construction in progress is the difference between consecutive balances; it does not measure cash spent on construction. Cash flow, capital expenditure and free cash flow are quarterly amounts. Capital expenditure includes property, plant and equipment and principal payments on finance leases.
